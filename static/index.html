Help — what to expect

Large PDFs (e.g. 2,000 pages)

  • Indexed page-by-page in the background (75+ pages). Wait until indexing finishes before relying on answers.
  • First-time indexing may take 10–30+ minutes depending on size and API speed.
  • Only the most relevant sections are sent to the AI per question — not the entire PDF.

Questions that work well

  • By topic: “Fireproofing requirements in Division 7”
  • By page: “What does page 842 say about concrete curing?”
  • Compare files: “Compare the P6 schedule with the contract PDF milestones.”

Limitations

  • Cannot summarize every page of a thousand-page set in one answer.
  • Scanned PDFs (image-only, no text) may index poorly until OCR is added — export searchable PDFs when possible.
  • Revit/DWG uploads are stored; use IFC or PDF exports for deeper analysis.
